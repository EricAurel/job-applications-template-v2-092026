## Team research before drafting

Before drafting any cover letter, scan the recipient organization's current team (careers/about pages, LinkedIn) for connections to the CV. Names of shared institutions, topics, or regions become single-sentence bridges in the letter.

## Closing argument before the warm sign-off

Cover letters should include a closing argument. One sentence near the end that explicitly summarises why the candidate is a good fit, placed before the warm personal sign-off.
